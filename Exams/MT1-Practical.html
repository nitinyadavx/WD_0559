Group Travels

Group travel is a great way to explore new destinations with friends, family, or like-minded travelers. It offers a sense of camaraderie and shared experiences that can make the journey even more memorable.

  • Objective: Synchronised deployment for collective operational success.
  • Execution: Multi-variable schedules aligned into a unified trajectory.
  • Advantage: Scaled resource efficiency, shared logistical frameworks, and networked dynamics.


Private Travels

Private travel allows you to customize your journey according to your preferences and schedule. Whether you're looking for a relaxing beach vacation or an adventurous mountain hike, private travel gives you the flexibility to create the perfect trip.

  • Objective: Absolute control over scheduling and routing variables.
  • Execution: Customised logistics isolating the client from mass transit friction.
  • Advantage: Unrestricted environmental control, schedule autonomy, and discreet movement.


Top Itineraries

  1. Agra: The Heritage Escape (2 Days / 1 Night)

    Take a short road trip via the Yamuna Expressway from Ghaziabad to Agra for a plunge into Mughal history.

    • Day 1: Arrive in Agra and check into your hotel. Spend your afternoon exploring the majestic Agra Fort and enjoy a sunset view of the Taj Mahal from Mehtab Bagh across the Yamuna River. End the evening trying authentic Mughlai cuisine.
    • Day 2: Wake up early for a spectacular sunrise visit to the Taj Mahal. Later, grab a hearty breakfast, shop for famous Agra marble handicrafts and Petha (a local sweet), and then head to the ancient city of Fatehpur Sikri on your way back home.

  2. Rishikesh: The Spiritual & Adventure Retreat (2 Days / 2 Nights)

    A perfect mix of peace and adrenaline, located just a few hours' drive from Ghaziabad in the foothills of the Himalayas.

    • Day 1: Arrive in Rishikesh and settle into a riverside camp or hotel. Spend the morning walking across the iconic suspension bridges and exploring local cafes. In the afternoon, head out for a thrilling white-water river rafting session on the Ganges. In the evening, witness the soul-stirring Ganga Aarti at Parmarth Niketan or Triveni Ghat.
    • Day 2: Start your day early with a peaceful yoga session or a short trek to the beautiful Neer Garh Waterfall. Grab breakfast at a famous bakery overlooking the river, do some souvenir shopping, and depart for home feeling completely rejuvenated.
